Does it hold up on sites the model has never seen?

The strongest earlier lead (relative band power) was tested on two new public cohorts. The test was written down and committed before their data were downloaded: logistic regression trained on ds007526 only must beat chance (95% interval above 0.50).

Train on three sites, test on the fourth (ADR 0016)

Leave-one-site-out: each site is held out once and predicted by a model that never saw it. All-feature logistic regression, external ROC-AUC. Pre-declared test (multi-site beats single-site at every site): not supported. Standardizing each site before pooling tends to help the two small sites. This is directional; the intervals overlap.

The 1/f slope, with peaks separated (ADR 0015)

PD-minus-control difference in the aperiodic exponent (Cohen's d). A plain line fit is pulled by the alpha peak; spectral parameterization models peaks separately. The effect shrinks once peaks are separated but stays clearly non-zero in the largest new cohort. The development cohort shows none, so it cannot teach a model this feature.
